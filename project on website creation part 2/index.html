About Prayga hotel

A beautiful place to stay

Prayag hotel, established in 1959, is a well-known budget-friendly hotel in Prayagraj (Allahabad), located opposite the railway station. It offers over 80 well-appointed rooms, an AC multicuisine restaurant, banquet facilities, and parking, making it suitable for business, family, and tourist stays. The hotel provides traditional hospitality, free Wi-Fi, and 24-hour room service


Key Information & Amenities

Location: 73 Noorullah Rd, Prayagraj, UP, 211003 (Near Railway Station). Room types: Offers Deluxe, Super Deluxe, and standard room options, including some with air conditioning. Dining: In-house AC multi-cuisine restaurant serving Indian and local delicacies. Amenities: Free Wi-Fi, free parking, elevator, CCTV surveillance, 24-hour front desk, and laundry services. Proximity: Less than 1 km from Allahabad Railway Station and 2 km from Civil Lines Bus Station.
